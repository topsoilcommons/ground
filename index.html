Ground

Local help, checked by hand. Food, shelter, and health care in New Rochelle and nearby Westchester.

The rule. A listing appears here only after a person checks it on the provider's own page. Every listing shows who checked it, the date, and the date to check again, 30 days later. A listing that passes that date is shown as stale, never silently. If a listing is wrong, the fix happens in the open, in this repository.

Verify a listing

This is the fastest way in. Pick one listing. Open the provider page it links to. Check that the offer, place, and times still match. Then open a pull request that adds your check to checks.json, with your name or handle and today's date. When it lands, the listing carries your name and the new date, and the board stays alive because of your minutes. The full steps are in the README of this repository.
