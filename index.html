Ground

Local help, checked by hand. Food, shelter, and health care in New Rochelle and nearby Westchester.

One of the seven tools of Topsoil Commons. Ground is Layer 0, the body.

First step: pick one listing below and tap Check this listing. The check takes about a minute.

The rule. A listing appears here only after a person checks it on the provider's own page. Every listing shows who checked it, the date, and the date to check again, 30 days later. A listing that passes that date is marked stale and stays on the board. If a listing is wrong, the fix happens in the open, in this repository.

How the counts and the checks are recorded

Generated by tools/build_counts.py. The counts come from the seed board entries in listings.json for the board on this page and places/index.json for the other places, both in this repository. Nothing in the counts section is typed by hand.

Each listing in listings.json carries its provider page in its source field, the date it was checked in its verified field, and the date to check again in its expires field, 30 days after the check. The public log of checks is checks.json in this repository: a check adds a row with the checker's name, stage name, or handle, and the new verified and expires dates. Full places record: PLACES.md in this repository. Full steps for checking a listing: the README of this repository, under Verify a listing.

Take away data: listings.json at this site root holds every Ground listing in one file, 160 listings across 19 places, each with its fields, its checked date, its expiry date, and its provider source. tools/build_listings.py builds that file from the seed board entries it keeps and the place files under places/, so the file and this page come from the same data.

The sample Proof on this page is the listing with the record name hope-food-pantry in listings.json, and its check is logged in checks.json.
