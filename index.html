Ground

Local help, checked by hand. Food, shelter, and health care in New Rochelle and nearby Westchester.

First step: pick one listing below and tap Check this listing. The check takes about a minute.

The rule. A listing appears here only after a person checks it on the provider's own page. Every listing shows who checked it, the date, and the date to check again, 30 days later. A listing that passes that date is marked stale and stays on the board. If a listing is wrong, the fix happens in the open, in this repository.

Ground in other places is recorded in PLACES.md in this repository, with each place checked the same way.

One listing, as a full Proof

Every listing on this board is a Proof. A Proof states what is offered, links to the provider page it came from, names who checked it, and gives the date they checked it.
