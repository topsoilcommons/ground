# Collaborate on Ground

Ground serves one need: the body. Food, shelter, health, safety. This page lists concrete, scoped ways an organization can work with the project. Nothing on this page implies an existing partnership; collaborations are invitations until they actually happen, and no partner is named publicly without their written agreement.

## Who this is for

Food pantries, meal programs, shelters, clinics, libraries, and the county and city offices that publish local service information in Westchester.

## Sized tasks

1. **Be listed, correctly.** If you run a service that belongs on this board, open an issue with the link to your own page that states the offer, place, and times. A maintainer checks it against that page and lists it with the check date showing.
2. **Correct your own listing.** If your hours or address change, open an issue or a pull request. Corrections to a listing are logged in checks.json like any other check.
3. **Adopt the board for your town.** Fork this repository, replace the listings with your area's, and keep the same rule: provider pages only, a check date and an expiry on every listing, stale shown in the open. Tell us, and your fork is linked here.

## What you get

A public, dated record: your listing with its check history, corrections logged beside it, in a repository anyone can audit.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository. Small and specific beats grand and vague.
